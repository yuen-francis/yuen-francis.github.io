## Research Interests

My research examines how children develop social and moral concepts.

### Moral Development

I am interested in how children understand and evaluate
moral behaviors, including the development of moral concepts
and moral language.

### Language Development

I investigate how children acquire and use evaluative and
moral language, including words such as *good* and *bad*.

### Social Cognition

I am also interested in children's understanding of
social interactions and other people's mental states.
